# Eligibility before career fit

An attractive skills score must never override a failed eligibility check. Unknown requirements are not proof of suitability or career progression. A fit percentage is not a hiring probability.

- Collect a numeric expected salary or comparable current salary, work authorization, sponsorship need, relocation preference and target roles before completing setup. Require birth date for Bangladesh; read explicit personal details from the uploaded CV first. Preserve manual edits, never derive age from career dates, and never copy DOB into a UK/US CV.
- Compare published compensation to the maximum of current salary, expected minimum and hard salary minimum, in the same country/currency/pay period. There is no 10% downward tolerance. Undisclosed pay or a range whose lower end is below the floor cannot receive Best/Strong Fit. Never treat the top of a government pay scale as guaranteed starting pay.
- Bangladesh: retain the circular's explicit age limits and calculation date. Missing cutoff, unknown birth date, age relaxation or incomplete source requirements require verification. A confirmed age mismatch excludes the vacancy from recommendations and blocks application preparation/sending.
- UK/US: ordinary job fit does not depend on age or protected personal characteristics. A posted age restriction is a source/occupational-rule review issue for every applicant, not automatic exclusion based on their DOB. Other markets remain review-required; regional CV conventions are not employment law.
- Respect current career level, verified experience, mandatory degrees/certifications/skills, work permission, explicit sponsorship, location/remote preferences and exclusions. Never infer employer prestige or a promotion from a name. Show the unmet requirement and provide a profile correction route where appropriate.
- The same eligibility checks run for matching, preparation, portal opening, confirmation and Gmail sending. Stored AI opinions cannot exceed deterministic ceilings. Profile/preference changes recheck existing matches without model calls. A source-only backfill preserves literal eligibility from existing descriptions and personal details from existing CVs.

## Reviewed primary guidance (23 October 2026)

- Bangladesh official vacancy source: https://alljobs.teletalk.com.bd/ (use the actual circular; no global assumption that all jobs have the same age limit).
- UK recruitment and DOB guidance: https://www.gov.uk/employer-preventing-discrimination/recruitment
- Northern Ireland guidance: https://www.nidirect.gov.uk/articles/age-discrimination
- US ADEA coverage and age protection: https://www.eeoc.gov/age-discrimination
- US prohibited hiring/referral practices and protected characteristics: https://www.eeoc.gov/prohibited-employment-policiespractices

No review of every country's employment law is claimed. New verified country/occupation exceptions require a source, effective date and regression coverage before automatic enforcement.
